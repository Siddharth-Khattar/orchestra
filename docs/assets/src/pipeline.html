New ids, and the entry's Seams: and Landing: lines
/orc-spec
The spec, citing its ids on a Registry: line
/orc-tickets
Tickets, each citing ids on a Satisfies: line
/clear
The build starts in a fresh session
/orc-build
Commits on spec/<name>, each with a Refs: trailer
/orc-discuss <name>if a decision is pending
Locks it as a new id, or rejects it: the rework becomes a new ticket through /orc-tickets, then /orc-build again
/orc-land
A merge commit on the default branch, tickets resolved

At any point, /orc shows where everything stands and /orc-next proposes the next step.
